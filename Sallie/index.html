Core Brand Colors

The primary colors that establish the foundation of Sallie brand identity. Used for major structural elements, primary buttons, and key branding moments.

Midnight Blue
#0C0034
The primary grounding color representing stability, warmth, and safety. Used for heavy typography and structural backgrounds.
Raspberry
#FF156B
The core brand highlight. Used to represent student individuality (e.g., the dot over the "i" in the logo) and to draw attention to key interactions.
Electric Blue
#004DE8
A vibrant, high-energy blue elevated for primary calls-to-action, core interactions, and strong graphic emphasis.

Accent Colors

Supporting colors utilized in illustrations, charts, iconography, and organic shape overlays to create a dynamic, non-linear visual path.

Soft Yellow
#FFE37D
A warm, approachable accent color used for energetic backgrounds, graphic shapes, and softening high-contrast layouts.
Clear Blue
#55D3FF
A fresh, airy blue perfect for gradients, backgrounds, and highlighting subtle ui components.
Orange Zest
#F06F1F
A lively, energetic accent used sparingly for notifications, badges, or illustration details.
Ultraviolet
#6238F8
A deep, creative purple that adds depth, contrast, and a modern digital feel to brand assets.

Semantic UI & Neutrals (Bootstrap)

Standardized UI feedback colors and foundational neutrals to ensure digital accessibility and standard user experience patterns.

UI Success
#28A745
Indicates successful actions, approvals, and positive trends (Green).
UI Danger
#DC3545
Indicates errors, destructive actions, or critical system alerts (Red).
UI Warning
#FFC107
Used for cautionary states, pending actions, and medium-priority alerts (Yellow).
UI Info
#17A2B8
Used for neutral information, tooltips, and contextual help (Cyan).
Neutral Black
#000000
Strict black for maximum contrast requirements and deepest shadows.
Neutral White
#FFFFFF
Primary background color for clean, readable layouts and surface areas.

Typography

Primary / Headlines (Roc Grotesk / Archivo Black)
Any student, any goal. Let's fund it.

Used for primary messaging, heroes, and major section titles. Conveys a strong, modern, and confident tone.

Body Copy (Open Sans / Arial)
Smart borrowing starts with knowing your options. Learn why comparing loans can help you make an informed decision. Whatever your goal, don’t pay more than you need to.

Highly legible, accessible sans-serif used for all standard body copy, ui components, labels, and instructional text.

Additional Surface Colors

Sallie's additional surface tokens and their usage & functional analysis.

Pure White
#FFFFFF (primary-1)
Default Canvas: Primary page background, form containers, and high-density financial disclosures requiring maximum readability.
Cool Off-White
#F7F7F9 (primary-2)
Subtle Surface Elevation: Alternating content bands, comparison tables, calculator panels, and card surfaces to establish visual hierarchy without harsh borders.
Electric Blue
#004DE8 (secondary-1)
Core Brand Action Surface: Primary CTA buttons, active navigation states, key conversion banners, and high-emphasis promotional sections.
Deep Royal Blue
#003AAE (secondary-2)
Interactive State & Depth: Hover/active states for components, sticky utility bars, and deep-contrast structural containers.
Ultraviolet
#6238F8 (accent-1)
Feature & Digital Product Highlights: Secondary hero banners, student resource hubs, and modular callouts distinguishing digital tools from core loan products.
Clear Blue
#55D3FF (accent-2)
High-Energy Graphic Overlays: Decorative shapes, progress indicators, and bold callout badges paired strictly with Midnight Blue typography.
Raspberry
#FF156B (accent-3)
Focal Point & Individuality: High-urgency badges, micro-highlights, and select brand moments echoing the dot over the "i" in the Sallie Mae wordmark.
Soft Yellow
#FFE37D (accent-4)
Warmth & Approachability: Highlight banners, promotional strips, and graphic backdrops designed to soften dense financial pages.
Light Yellow Tint
#FFF1C0 (tertiary-1)
Low-Saturation Informational Container: Tinted version of Soft Yellow; used for editorial callout boxes, rate-disclaimer notices, and multi-line text containers.
Ice Blue Tint
#EAF9FF (tertiary-2)
Soft Informational / Feature Card: Tinted version of Clear Blue; used for "Did You Know" tips, FAQ containers, and gentle visual grouping.
Soft Lavender Tint
#D4CBF2 (tertiary-3)
Editorial & Tool Grouping: Tinted version of Ultraviolet; used for secondary feature cards, student-life content blocks, and multi-column grid backgrounds.
